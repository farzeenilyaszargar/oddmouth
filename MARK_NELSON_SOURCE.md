## Reliability and remaining checks

The recordings are linked from the original LibriVox catalogue, have chapter listings and online-text links, and identify production/proof-listening credits. This makes the source traceable. It does not make the recordings studio masters or the text sentence-aligned.

If the user approves the voice and later authorizes acquisition, use the original publisher links rather than arbitrary YouTube reuploads that may add music, compression, speed changes, or effects. Check chapters from different books and recording years for timbre consistency, clipping, noise, reverberation, microphone changes, and character impressions. Preserve unprocessed originals and provenance.

The source pages identify the recordings as **public domain in the USA** and ask users elsewhere to check local status. That designation is not proof of an explicit synthetic-voice permission or worldwide rights for every intended use. Neither has been established here. The source is free to access, not certified as an unrestricted commercial cloning licence.
